The house

The Canonicus is our summer oceanfront rental on Moody Beach (a private beach) in Wells, Maine. This 3 bedroom, 1.5 bath house sits halfway between two of the few public ways on Moody, so you get a quieter private-beach stretch with direct stairs to the sand.

Canonicus (pronounced kuh-NON-ih-kus) is named after a Narragansett sachem (chief) who was a prominent leader in the early 17th century and maintained peaceful relations with English settlers in the region.
